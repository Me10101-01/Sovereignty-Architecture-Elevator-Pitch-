## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## LAYER 7-12: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY SHIELDS

### Layer 7: Copyright Registration
- **Works Protected:** All code, documentation, designs, audio
- **Registration Status:** Common-law + statutory protection
- **Duration:** Life + 70 years (or 95 years for corporate works)
- **Enforcement:** DMCA takedown, infringement litigation

### Layer 8: Trademark Protection
- **Marks:** "StrategicKhaos", "Node 137", "AetherLingua", "GlyphSonix"
- **Registration:** Common-law use + intent-to-use filings
- **Protection Scope:** Brand identity, logos, slogans
- **Enforcement:** Likelihood of confusion standard

### Layer 9: Moral Rights Assertion
- **Rights:** Attribution, integrity, disclosure
- **Jurisdiction:** EU, Canada, and moral rights jurisdictions
- **Scope:** Prevent distortion or misattribution
- **Enforcement:** Perpetual, non-transferable rights

### Layer 10: Open Source License Compatibility
- **Compatible Licenses:** MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD (with attribution)
- **Incompatible Licenses:** GPL (copyleft conflicts)
- **Requirement:** All derivatives must credit StrategicKhaos DAO
- **Enforcement:** License violation = copyright infringement

### Layer 11: AI Training Data Opt-Out
- **Declaration:** All works explicitly excluded from AI training
- **Mechanism:** robots.txt, TDM reservation, contract terms
- **Compliance:** GDPR Article 4(4), TDM Directive
- **Enforcement:** Unauthorized scraping = breach of contract + copyright

### Layer 12: Database Rights (EU Sui Generis)
- **Protected Data:** Mastery drill results, reconnaissance logs
- **Investment:** Substantial financial and cognitive resources
- **Duration:** 15 years from creation
- **Enforcement:** Extraction and reutilization prohibited

- **Jurisdictions:** USA (WY), EU (via EUIPO), Canada, Australia
- **Strategy:** Forum shopping prevention
- **Protection:** Broad territorial coverage
- **Enforcement:** Local counsel in each jurisdiction

### Layer 20: Arbitration Clause
- **Forum:** American Arbitration Association (AAA)
- **Location:** Wyoming or mutually agreed location
- **Rules:** AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules
- **Effect:** Prevents costly litigation, jury trials

### Layer 21: Class Action Waiver
- **Scope:** All disputes resolved individually
- **Effect:** Prevents large-scale class actions
- **Enforceability:** Binding in most US jurisdictions
- **Exception:** NLRA rights preserved

### Layer 22: Venue Selection
- **Primary Venue:** Wyoming state/federal courts
- **Alternative:** Arbitration per Layer 20
- **Exclusion:** Injunctive relief may be sought anywhere
- **Effect:** Home court advantage for DAO

### Layer 23: Choice of Law
- **Governing Law:** Wyoming Revised Statutes
- **Conflict of Laws:** Wyoming choice-of-law rules apply
- **Exceptions:** IP laws of jurisdiction where infringement occurs
- **Effect:** Predictable legal framework

### Layer 24: Sovereign Immunity Analog
- **Concept:** DAO operates as quasi-sovereign entity
- **Mechanism:** Wyoming DAO shield + decentralization
- **Enforcement:** Members shielded from personal liability
- **Limitation:** Does not extend to criminal acts

## ENFORCEMENT MECHANISMS

### Automatic License Termination
Upon any violation of terms, all licenses automatically terminate without notice.

### Graduated Response
1. **First Offense:** Cease & desist letter
2. **Second Offense:** DMCA takedown + public notice
3. **Third Offense:** Litigation + damages

### Injunctive Relief
Immediate court orders to prevent irreparable harm to DAO's IP.

### Damages & Penalties
- **Statutory Damages:** Up to $150,000 per work (copyright)
- **Actual Damages:** Proven losses + infringer's profits
- **Punitive Damages:** For willful infringement (where available)
- **Attorney's Fees:** Prevailing party recovers costs
