## TRADE SECRET PROTECTION

### Definition
Trade secrets are confidential business information that provides competitive advantage, including:

#### Technical Secrets
- Proprietary algorithms (not publicly disclosed)
- Optimization techniques
- Performance benchmarks (internal)
- Infrastructure configurations (sensitive)
- Security vulnerabilities discovered (pre-disclosure)

#### Business Secrets
- Customer lists and relationships
- Pricing strategies and financial models
- Marketing plans and strategies
- Strategic partnerships and negotiations

### Protection Measures

#### Access Control
1. **Repository Access:** Private repos for sensitive code
2. **Encrypted Storage:** AES-256-GCM or stronger
3. **Role-Based Permissions:** Least privilege principle
4. **Multi-Factor Authentication:** Required for all access

#### Legal Measures
1. **Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs):** Required for collaborators
2. **Confidentiality Clauses:** In all contracts
3. **Trade Secret Designation:** Clear marking of confidential materials
4. **Employee/Contractor Agreements:** IP assignment + confidentiality

#### Technical Measures
1. **Encryption at Rest:** All sensitive data encrypted
2. **Encryption in Transit:** TLS 1.3+ for all communications
3. **Code Obfuscation:** For production deployments (when needed)
4. **Watermarking:** Steganographic marks in code

### Reasonable Secrecy Measures
To maintain trade secret protection, we implement:
- Written confidentiality policies
- Access logs and audit trails
- Periodic security reviews
- Incident response procedures
- Employee training on confidentiality

### Enforcement
**Uniform Trade Secrets Act (UTSA):** Civil remedies  
**Economic Espionage Act (EEA):** Criminal penalties for theft  
**Defend Trade Secrets Act (DTSA):** Federal civil cause of action

## MORAL RIGHTS

### European & Canadian Law
In jurisdictions recognizing moral rights (EU, Canada, etc.):

#### Right of Attribution
- Authors must be credited by name
- False attribution is prohibited
- Attribution cannot be removed without consent

#### Right of Integrity
- Works cannot be distorted, mutilated, or modified in ways that harm author's reputation
- Destruction of works may violate integrity right
- Context matters (parody, criticism may be permitted)

#### Right of Disclosure
- Author controls first publication
- Author decides when work is "complete"
- Premature disclosure violates moral rights

#### Right of Withdrawal
- Author may withdraw work from circulation (rarely exercised)
- Must compensate any economic harm to licensees
- Applies mainly to fine arts, not software (practical limits)

### USA Law (Limited)
**Visual Artists Rights Act (VARA):** Applies to fine art only, not software  
**Common-Law Moral Rights:** Very limited recognition  
**Contractual Moral Rights:** Can be negotiated in licenses

### Our Assertion
StrategicKhaos DAO LLC asserts moral rights to the fullest extent recognized in any applicable jurisdiction, including:
- Requirement to credit "StrategicKhaos DAO LLC" and "Node 137"
- Prohibition on misattribution or false authorship claims
- Protection against modifications that harm our reputation

## ENFORCEMENT MECHANISMS

### Detection & Monitoring

#### Automated
- GitHub code search
- Dependency graph analysis
- Web scraping for unauthorized copies
- Trademark monitoring services

#### Manual
- Community reports (Discord, GitHub issues)
- Periodic Google searches
- Legal database queries (USPTO, EUIPO)

### Graduated Response

#### Level 1: Friendly Notice
- Cease & desist letter (friendly tone)
- Explain violation
- Offer license if appropriate
- 30-day cure period

#### Level 2: Formal Demand
- Cease & desist from legal counsel
- DMCA takedown notices (for hosted content)
- Public notice of violation (GitHub issue, social media)
- 14-day cure period

#### Level 3: Legal Action
- File copyright infringement lawsuit
- Seek injunctive relief (immediate stop)
- Claim damages (statutory, actual, infringer's profits)
- Recover attorney's fees and costs

### Remedies Available

#### Injunctive Relief
- Temporary restraining order (TRO)
- Preliminary injunction
- Permanent injunction
- Specific performance (contract claims)

#### Monetary Damages
**Copyright:**
- Statutory damages: $750 - $30,000 per work (or up to $150,000 if willful)
- Actual damages + infringer's profits
- Attorney's fees for prevailing party

**Trademark:**
- Actual damages + infringer's profits
- Treble damages for willful infringement
- Statutory damages for cybersquatting: $1,000 - $100,000

**Trade Secrets:**
- Actual loss + unjust enrichment
- Reasonable royalty
- Exemplary damages (up to 2× actual damages)

**Contract:**
- Expectation damages
- Reliance damages
- Restitution

#### Equitable Remedies
- Accounting of profits
- Constructive trust
- Disgorgement of gains

## EXCEPTIONS & LIMITATIONS

### Fair Use (USA)
Four factors:
1. Purpose and character of use (transformative? commercial?)
2. Nature of copyrighted work (factual vs. creative)
3. Amount and substantiality used
4. Effect on market value

**Our Position:** Fair use is welcome for commentary, criticism, education, parody. Not for commercial AI training.

### Fair Dealing (Commonwealth)
Categories vary by jurisdiction, typically:
- Research and private study
- Criticism and review
- News reporting
- Parody and satire

### EU Exceptions
- TDM for scientific research (non-commercial)
- Quotation and criticism
- Parody and pastiche
- Private copying (with levy)
